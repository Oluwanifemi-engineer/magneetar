# Magneetar — Tester Recruitment Messages (WhatsApp-ready)

Copy-paste, personalize the bracketed bits, and send. Honesty is the brand:
this app lives or dies on trust, so the message must never read like a promo
or a scam — real name, real purpose, no urgency tricks, no "rewards".

---

## A. WhatsApp group message (primary)

> Hi everyone 👋 — this is [YOUR NAME].
>
> I built an Android anti-theft app called **Magneetar** — it tracks your
> phone, alerts you the moment your SIM is swapped, lets you lock/wipe it
> remotely from a web dashboard, and captures evidence (photo + audio)
> during a theft response. It's been tested against every automated check I
> have, but I refuse to put it on the Play Store until real people have used
> it on real phones under real conditions — that's where the honest bugs
> live.
>
> So I'm recruiting **real testers**:
> - **What you do:** install the app from magneetar.me/download, keep it on
>   your phone and use it normally for 2 weeks (grant it location access —
>   that's the whole point), and give me 10 minutes of feedback once a week.
> - **What you get:** a direct line to fix issues as we find them, and the
>   satisfaction of being among the first to trust your phone to it. If your
>   feedback changes the product, your name goes in the credits.
> - **What I need from you:** an honest account of anything that feels wrong
>   — battery drain, missed alerts, the app dying in the background, anything.
>
> If you're in (ideally people with Tecno/Infinix/Itel, Samsung, Xiaomi, or
> Redmi phones), just say "in" and I'll send the install + setup steps
> privately. Questions welcome — nothing is off limits.

## B. One-to-one follow-up (after they say "in")

> Thanks for signing up! Here's the 3-step start:
>
> 1. Open **magneetar.me/download** on your phone → download the APK →
>    install. If Google Play Protect shows a warning, tap "More details" →
>    "Install anyway" (or pause Play Protect scanning in
>    Settings → Security → Google Play Protect → gear icon → off, then
>    install). This warning exists because the app is NOT on the Play Store
>    yet — that's exactly what this test is for.
> 2. Open the app → create an account (email + password) → grant **location
>    access (allow all the time)** when asked → follow the setup steps.
> 3. Keep the app installed and use your phone normally for 2 weeks.
>
> Weekly check-in: I'll send you a short form (5 minutes, WhatsApp-friendly
> questions). If anything feels off in between — battery draining fast, no
> location updates, weird alerts — just message me directly with what you
> saw and when.
>
> One important note: this is a real anti-theft app, so it runs in the
> background and collects location data that goes ONLY to your own account
> (visible to you on the dashboard at app.magneetar.me). It's never shared
> or sold. If you ever want it gone, Delete Account is in the dashboard
> settings.
>
> Questions? Ask me anything.

## C. Mid-window nudge (day ~4, group)

> Quick check-in for the Magneetar testers — how's it going? Any battery
> surprises, missed alerts, or phones that went quiet in the background?
> Good news is data too (tell me what's working). No rush — answers in by
> the weekend if you can.

## D. End-of-window wrap-up (day ~15, individual)

> Your 2-week test window is done — thank you! 🙏 One last form (5 minutes)
> and I'd love a plain verdict: would you keep this app on your phone /
> recommend it to someone who's had a phone stolen? Your answer, plus the
> bug reports, decide whether Magneetar goes to the Play Store.
>
> If you're willing to keep it installed a bit longer for the next phase
> (Google's official closed testing), say the word — you'd be a founding
> tester there too.

## Notes for the sender

- **Personalize** the bracketed bits; a message that looks mass-sent gets
  ignored.
- **Recruit ≥5 for G1 and ≥12 for G2** — the G1 cohort rolls straight into
  G2 (Play closed testing), so ask the keepers to stay on.
- **Never over-promise.** No rewards, no "free gift", no urgency. The pitch
  is: honest early access + shaping the product + credits.
- The Guardian Network is literally built for this — users who already trust
  the app's premise are the most natural testers.
- The install warning (Play Protect) WILL appear for everyone; section B
  handles it honestly instead of hiding it.
